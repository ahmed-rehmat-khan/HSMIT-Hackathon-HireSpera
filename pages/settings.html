What these rules touch

One source of truth keeps the numbers honest across the whole workspace.

  • Late is measured against the start time plus grace.
  • Under five worked hours becomes a Half Day.
  • Only approved leave reduces the yearly balance.
  • Per-day pay divides salary by the working days in that month.
